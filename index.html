Why Choose Us

The Sunnyway Wendy Difference

We build structures that last — with materials and craftsmanship you can trust.

Sustainable

Built from renewable timber with a significantly lower carbon footprint than brick or concrete.

Comfortable

How We Work

Our Build Process

A clear, structured process from first contact to finished structure.

01

Consultation & Design

We start by understanding your needs, site and budget — then craft a design that works for you.

02

Timber Selection & Treatment

The right timber is selected and professionally treated before any construction begins.

03

Foundation Preparation

A solid, level foundation is laid to ensure structural stability from the ground up.

04

Log Assembly & Roofing

The timber frame goes up, walls are assembled, and roofing is fitted to spec.

05

Finishing & Handover

All finishing work is completed and a full quality inspection is done before handover.
