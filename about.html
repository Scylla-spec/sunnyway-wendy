About Sunnyway Wendy

Who we are and what drives us to build better.

Our Story

At Sunnyway Wendy, we believe that quality housing should be accessible to everyone. We design and construct log timber houses that combine natural beauty, durability, and affordability — giving families, landlords, and businesses a smarter way to build.

From backyard rooms and granny flats to site offices and full residential homes, we bring craftsmanship and care to every structure we build across Zimbabwe and South Africa.

Our Mission

To design and construct eco-friendly log homes that meet modern living standards while preserving the natural character of wood. We are committed to sustainability, quality craftsmanship, and structures that stand the test of time.

What Are Log Timber Houses?

🌲

Solid Timber Construction

Built using high-quality solid wood logs as the main structural material — not brick, not concrete.

🌡️

Natural Insulation

Wood regulates indoor temperature naturally, keeping your space comfortable year-round.

🎨

Unique Aesthetic

Every structure has its own natural grain and texture — warm, real, and one of a kind.

Our Commitment to Quality

We use professionally treated timber, modern construction techniques, and skilled craftsmanship to ensure every structure meets safety, durability, and aesthetic standards. Every build is inspected before handover — no shortcuts, no compromises.
